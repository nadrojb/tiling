Wall Tiling

Bathroom walls, shower enclosures and kitchen splashbacks — ceramic and porcelain tiles fitted to British Standards across Wiltshire.

From bathrooms and showers to kitchen splashbacks and feature walls, I provide wall tiling for homes across Wiltshire, Bath and the surrounding areas.

Every wall tiling project starts with the preparation. Before any tiles are fixed, I assess the surface and make sure it is suitable for tiling. Where required, this can include repairing uneven areas, installing suitable backer boards or preparing wet areas with a tanking system.

You can read more about the different preparation methods I use here , and tiling over plywood here .

Once the surface is ready, I take care with the setting-out so the finished tiles look balanced and work well around corners, windows, niches and other features. Whether you're using small metro tiles or larger format porcelain, I aim for clean lines, consistent grout joints and a finish that looks right from every angle.

I work with ceramic, porcelain and natural stone tiles, and can accommodate a range of tile sizes, patterns and layouts.
