Regrouting & Tile Repairs in Trowbridge

Over time, grout can crack, discolour or crumble. Silicone degrades. Tiles can crack or come loose. These are common issues that can be fixed without retiling the entire room.

I offer regrouting, silicone replacement and individual tile repairs to refresh the look of your existing tiled surfaces — kitchen, bathroom or floor.

Regrouting

Old grout removed and replaced with fresh grout. Available in different colours to match or update the look of your tiles.

Tile Replacement

Individual cracked or damaged tiles replaced where matching tiles are available. Careful removal to avoid damaging surrounding tiles.

Silicone Replacement

Old, mouldy or degraded silicone removed and replaced with fresh sanitary grade silicone in bathrooms and kitchens.
