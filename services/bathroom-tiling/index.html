A bathroom needs to look good, but it also needs to cope with water, moisture and daily use. I take care of the full process — from preparing the walls and floors, through to tiling, grouting and finishing.

Whether you're having a full bathroom refitted or just updating the tiles, I'll make sure the preparation is done properly and the finished result is clean, precise and built to last.

Wall Tiling

Ceramic and porcelain wall tiles for bathrooms, shower areas and behind baths. Prepared, set out and fixed with attention to detail and clean finishing.

Floor Tiling

Ceramic and porcelain floor tiles for bathroom floors. Substrate assessed, levelled where required, and tiled to a flat, consistent finish.

Shower Areas

Full shower enclosure tiling including tanking and waterproofing behind the tiles. A proper waterproof barrier protects the structure and prevents future problems.

Preparation & Waterproofing

Preparation is the part most people don't see, but it's what makes the difference between a bathroom that looks good for a year and one that lasts. Before tiling, I assess the walls and floors, repair any issues, and apply waterproofing (tanking) in shower areas and wet zones.

Tiles, adhesive and grout alone should not be relied upon to waterproof a shower. A properly installed tanking system creates a waterproof barrier behind the tiles, preventing water from reaching the structure underneath.
