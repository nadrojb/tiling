What I Offer

Preparation Done Right

Good tiling starts long before the first tile goes down. Proper preparation is the difference between a finish that lasts and one that doesn't. Here's how I make sure every job starts right.

Surface Assessment

Before any work begins, I check the condition of your walls and floors. I'm looking for cracks, damp, uneven areas, and anything that could affect the finished result. If there's a problem, I'll let you know before we go any further.

Tile Removal

If you've got old tiles that need to come up, I'll strip them out cleanly and dispose of everything. I remove adhesive residue and leave the surface ready for the next stage.

Levelling & Floating

Uneven floors cause tiles to crack and lippage. I use levelling compounds and floating screeds to create a flat, stable surface that's ready for tiling — whether it's a new build or an older property.

Tanking & Waterproofing

Wet rooms, showers, and any area exposed to water need proper tanking. I apply waterproof membranes and tanking systems to protect your walls and floors from moisture damage long-term.
