Floor Tiling in Trowbridge & Wiltshire

Floor tiling is a durable, hard-wearing choice for kitchens, bathrooms, hallways and living areas. I lay porcelain, ceramic and natural stone floor tiles, with the substrate assessed and prepared before any tiling begins.

An uneven or unsuitable floor can cause problems with tile adhesion, lippage and long-term durability. I check the floor before starting and carry out levelling or preparation where needed to create a suitable surface for tiling.

Porcelain Floor Tiles

Dense, durable and resistant to stains and scratches. Porcelain is a popular choice for high-traffic areas and works well in kitchens, bathrooms and hallways.

Ceramic Floor Tiles

A good option for lighter-use areas. Ceramic is easier to cut and work with, making it a practical choice for many floor tiling projects.

Natural Stone

Natural stone tiles bring a unique look to any room. Each tile is different, giving a floor real character. Stone may require sealing after installation.

Floor Preparation

Before tiling a floor, I check that the substrate is sound, flat and suitable for the tile being installed. Where required, I use self-levelling compound to correct uneven areas and create a consistent surface. For timber substrates, I may use plywood, backer boards or decoupling membranes to provide a stable base for tiling.

Getting the floor preparation right is essential for achieving a flat, long-lasting tiled floor. Skipping this step can lead to cracked tiles, uneven grout lines and poor adhesion.
