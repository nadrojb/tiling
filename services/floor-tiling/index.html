Floor Tiling

Porcelain, ceramic and natural stone floor tiles for kitchens, bathrooms, hallways and living areas across Wiltshire.

I provide floor tiling for bathrooms, kitchens, hallways, utility rooms and living spaces across Wiltshire, Bath and the surrounding areas.

A good tiled floor starts with a suitable, stable and properly prepared substrate. Before tiling begins, I assess the existing floor and determine what preparation is needed. Depending on the substrate and its condition, this may include repairs, backer boards, self-levelling compound or a suitable decoupling system.

You can read more about the different preparation methods I use here .

Each floor is carefully set out to achieve the best finish possible.

I work with ceramic, porcelain and natural stone floor tiles, including large-format tiles and a variety of patterns and layouts.
