Splashback Tiling in Trowbridge & Wiltshire

A kitchen splashback is one of the most visible parts of a kitchen. It needs to look clean, with precise cuts around sockets, worktops and edges. I tile splashbacks using ceramic and porcelain tiles, with neat finishing using trims or silicone where needed.

Whether you're fitting a brand new kitchen or updating the tiles behind your worktops, I'll make sure the set-out is symmetrical, the cuts are clean and the finished result looks right.

Ceramic Splashbacks

Lightweight, easy to clean and available in a wide range of colours and finishes. Ceramic is the most popular choice for kitchen splashbacks.

Porcelain Splashbacks

Denser and more durable than ceramic. Porcelain splashbacks are a great option if you want something hard-wearing with a modern look.

Cuts & Finishing

Neat cuts around plug sockets, extractor fans and worktop trims. Clean edges and a tidy finish that looks professional up close.
