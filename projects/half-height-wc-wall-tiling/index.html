Half-Height WC Wall Tiling in Trowbridge

300 × 70mm ceramic wall tiles laid in a brick-bond pattern to half-height, as part of my wall tiling work in Trowbridge. Walls were plumbed prior to installation, with a wooden trim fitted to the top edge. Finished with Mapei Ultracolor 132 grout and matching silicone.

Project Details

Location Trowbridge, Wiltshire
Material 300 x 70mm ceramic
Area 3m²
Pattern Brick bond
