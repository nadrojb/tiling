Taking pride in every job, big or small

I started Westway Tiling because I believe good work still matters. Whether it's a full kitchen floor or a small bathroom splashback, I treat every job the same — I take my time, do it properly, and don't cut corners.

Tiling is a skill I've built up over time and I genuinely enjoy it. There's something satisfying about transforming a room with good quality work. I'm based in Trowbridge and cover Wiltshire, Bath and Bristol.

I know finding a tradesman you can trust isn't easy. That's why I keep things straightforward — clear pricing, no hard sell, and I always clean up after myself. If you're not happy, I'm not happy. Simple as that.
